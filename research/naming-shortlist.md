## Recommendation

1. **Last Course** if you want the lowest-risk evocative name. Buy lastcourse.co now and make an offer on lastcourse.com later if the business works.
2. **Aftermint** if you want the most memorable name and are willing to run a USPTO search (or pay a trademark attorney for a one-hour clearance) before printing cards.
3. **Tablenote** is a safe fallback and is already wired into the site.

Names to avoid: anything with "Google", "5 stars" or "star" in it (policy and trademark exposure), and the brandable coinages with a near-identical existing brand (Plaudo, Encor, Kudoz).

## Before you commit to any name

- Search the USPTO trademark database for the exact word and close variants in classes 9, 35 and 42.
- Register the .co (or .com if open) plus a short redirect domain for the cards (4 to 6 characters if possible; the card URL is permanent once a tag is locked).
- Check the Instagram and TikTok handles.
- Say "drop the ___ card" out loud to a server and see if it needs explaining.
