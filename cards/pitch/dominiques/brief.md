## Before you walk in

- Only one Dominique's exists near Stonecrest; it sits in the former Arizona's building beside The Mall at Stonecrest, off I-20 exit 75.
- Some listings still say Lithonia, GA 30038.
- Parking is the restaurant's own lot on the mall outparcel.
- Hours per the official site: Mon-Thu 12-10pm, Fri-Sat 12-11pm, Sun 12-9pm; lunch Mon-Fri 12-3pm; brunch Sat-Sun 12-3pm.
- Closed Labor Day, Monday Sept 7, 2026.
- Avoid Friday evenings (live band) and weekend brunch; a weekday between 3pm and 5pm is the quietest window.
- Happy hour runs afternoons into 7pm; the owner's Google post says 4-7pm while reviews mention 3-7pm.
- No owner or manager name appears on the business's own site or social pages, so ask for the owner or general manager.
- Phone (470) 837-7617; Facebook also lists (470) 679-3170; email Info@dominiquesatstonecrest.com.
- The site runs on Popmenu and shows a $500 drawing for on-site dish reviews (drawing 10/1).
- Do not tie the NFC card to that drawing; Google prohibits incentivized reviews.
- Roughly 68% of the newest 60 Google reviews name a server, often posted within minutes on the same night, so staff already ask at the table.
- None of the newest 60 Google reviews had an owner reply, including a 1-star posted today about a tip charge.
- Third-party hours conflict on Yelp, EatOkra and Restaurantji, which is a listings-cleanup talking point.
- Google flags the listing as Black-owned and women-owned, price $30-90 per person.
- Social: Facebook @dominiquesstonecrest, Instagram @dominiquesatstonecrest, TikTok @dominiquesatstonecrest.
- Logo is a black script wordmark; if the pack needs a dark color use #101010, with #E6D0A9 as the gold accent.

- Review velocity measured Aug 13 to Sep 5 2026 is about 66 to 76 a month, so say 'about 70', never '50'.
